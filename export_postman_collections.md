How to Export a Postman Collection (Cisco SD-WAN Example)

Step 1

Open Postman and go to the Collections panel.

Step 2

Locate your Cisco SD-WAN collection. For example, a collection
containing these requests: - Login (POST /j_security_check) -
Get Devices (GET /dataservice/device)

Step 3

Click the three dots (⋮) next to the Cisco SD-WAN collection.

Step 4

Select Export.

Step 5

Choose Collection v2.1 (Recommended) as the export format.

Step 6

Click Export.

Step 7

Choose a location on your computer and save the file, for example:

Cisco_SDWAN_API_Collection.json

Step 8

Verify the exported collection by importing the JSON file into Postman.
You should see your Cisco SD-WAN requests, including: - Login
(POST /j_security_check) - Get Devices (GET /dataservice/device)

Done! Your Cisco SD-WAN Postman collection has been exported and is
ready to back up, share, or import into another Postman workspace.
